## 2026-07-14 - Bottom Tab Bar & FAB UX Refinement
**Learning:** Pairing 'aria-current="page"' with visual active states in navigation bars significantly improves accessibility for screen readers. Tactile feedback like 'active:scale-95' combined with a general 'transition' (rather than 'transition-colors') makes the UI feel more responsive and "app-like".
**Action:** Always ensure navigation buttons have 'aria-current' and primary actions have scale-based tactile feedback with smooth transitions.
