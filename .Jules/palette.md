## 2024-05-14 - [Tactile Feedback & Navigation Accessibility]
**Learning:** Adding `active:scale-95` provides immediate tactile feedback on mobile-first interfaces, making the app feel more responsive. Pairing this with `aria-current="page"` ensures that both visual and screen-reader users have a clear understanding of their current location.
**Action:** Always include `active:scale-95` (or similar) and appropriate ARIA labels/states for primary navigation elements to improve both "delight" and accessibility.
