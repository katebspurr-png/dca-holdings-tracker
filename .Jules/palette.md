## 2025-07-04 - [Bottom Nav & FAB Tactile Feedback]
**Learning:** Subtle tactile feedback (active:scale-95) in navigation elements and primary actions significantly improves the "native feel" of a mobile-first web application.
**Action:** Always include 'transition' and 'active:scale-95' on primary navigation tabs and floating action buttons to provide immediate visual confirmation of touch/click intent.
