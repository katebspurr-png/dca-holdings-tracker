## 2026-03-26 - [Holding Form Autofocus, Accessible Labeling and Tactile Transitions]
**Learning:** When modals containing a primary text input field are opened, lacking automatic focus on the primary field causes cognitive friction and extra tap/click interactions. Additionally, secondary controls (like dropdown exchange selectors) nested inside field layouts are often overlooked by screen readers unless explicitly labeled. Furthermore, interactive buttons must consistently leverage transition states paired with active scaling to maintain tactile harmony across the app.
**Action:** Always include the autoFocus property on the main input field of any newly opened dialog. Ensure all custom/UI select triggers have clear ARIA label descriptors, and always apply standard active:scale-95 transitions alongside themed focus-visible ring offsets for interactive triggers.
