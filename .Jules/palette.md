## 2025-06-27 - [BottomTabBar Accessibility and Tactile Feedback]
**Learning:** Implementing 'aria-current="page"' on active navigation buttons is critical for screen reader users to identify their location. Additionally, subtle tactile feedback like 'active:scale-95' significantly improves perceived responsiveness.
**Action:** Always include 'aria-current' and tactile 'active' states in navigation components.
