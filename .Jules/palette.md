## 2025-05-15 - Enhancing Mobile Interactivity and Accessibility
**Learning:** Subtle tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) in navigation elements significantly improves perceived responsiveness in mobile-first applications. Additionally, `aria-current="page"` is critical for screen readers to identify the active context in custom tab bars.
**Action:** Consistently apply `active:scale-95` and `transition` to primary action buttons and navigation tabs. Ensure active states are communicated semantically.
