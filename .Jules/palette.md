## 2025-06-27 - Tactile Feedback & ARIA Current in Mobile-First Navigation
**Learning:** Subtle tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) in navigation elements like the BottomTabBar significantly improves perceived responsiveness and user confidence in mobile-first applications. Additionally, implementing `aria-current="page"` is critical for screen reader users to identify their location.
**Action:** Always include `active:scale` transforms and `aria-current` attributes for primary navigation components.
