## 2025-05-15 - [Tactile Feedback & Accessible Hit Areas]
**Learning:** Adding 'active:scale-95' to custom buttons provides immediate tactile feedback that enhances the perceived quality of the app, especially on mobile. Additionally, replacing spans with associated 'label' elements for toggles significantly improves accessibility and hit area usability without changing the visual design.
**Action:** Always check custom (non-component) interactive elements for missing tactile feedback and ensuring form controls have semantic labels with 'htmlFor' association.
