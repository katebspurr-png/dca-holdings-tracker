## 2025-08-01 - Accessible and Tactile Navigation Buttons
**Learning:** Back buttons and primary navigation controls should always combine explicit ARIA labels (e.g., "Back to scenarios page" rather than just "Back") with delightful active:scale-95 scaling transitions and custom offset focus-visible ring styles for robust keyboard navigability and tactile screen-reader feedback.
**Action:** Always verify back buttons on subpages and detail views have explicit context-specific ARIA labels, smooth scale transitions, and custom offset focus rings matching the design system token styles.
