## 2026-07-20 - Custom Segment Selectors Accessibility & Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** Custom button-based segmented controls (like Default Exchange or Theme Toggle) offer beautiful interfaces but frequently omit ARIA roles and keyboard focus styles, making them completely inaccessible to keyboard and screen reader users. Proper pairing of `active:scale-95 transition` tactile feedback with semantic grouping (`role="radiogroup"`, `role="radio"`, `aria-checked`, and focus-visible rings) delivers both delight and robust accessibility.
**Action:** When replacing standard select menus with custom button segments, always group with `role="radiogroup"`, map individual buttons with `role="radio"` + `aria-checked={isActive}`, apply custom focus-ring states matching the design tokens (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-stitch-accent/50 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-stitch-bg focus:outline-none`), and pair with smooth active scaling animations.
