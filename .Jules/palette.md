## 2026-03-26 - [Bottom Tab Bar UX and Accessibility]
**Learning:** In mobile-first layouts, bottom navigation bars are the primary tactile touchpoint. Raw state changes on tab items without transitions feel jarring to users, and missing keyboard navigation states (focus indicators) combined with absent screen-reader role information (like `aria-current`) exclude power users and disabled users alike. Pairing smooth visual scaling animations (`active:scale-95 transition`) with explicit ARIA current page tags and themed focus offsets provides a comprehensive and delightful micro-UX improvement.
**Action:** Always combine interactive scaling animations with general transitions rather than color-only transitions, and pair them with explicit ARIA current identifiers and themed focus ring states.
