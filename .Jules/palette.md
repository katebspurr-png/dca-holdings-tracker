## 2026-10-30 - Accessible Fallbacks & Focus States on Scenario Detail Page
**Learning:** Fallback error states (e.g. non-existent items/404s) can leave users stuck if they lack clear navigation actions, and icon buttons in detail headers benefit from explicit `aria-label` descriptors and design system focus rings.
**Action:** Always provide action recovery controls (such as a 'Back to list' button) on fallback pages and ensure header navigation buttons have `aria-label` attributes and focus-visible offset ring styling.
