## 2026-08-08 - Accessible and Tactile Custom Segmented/Radio Controls
**Learning:** In Tailwind and shadcn-based custom component designs (like the default exchange selector or questionnaire option lists), standard semantic `<button>` elements can easily be made accessible and visually delightful for keyboard and assistive technology users. We can achieve this by styling them with design system-aligned focus offsets (`focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-stitch-accent/50 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-stitch-bg focus:outline-none`) combined with tactile physical scaling responses (`active:scale-[0.98] transition-all duration-150`). Additionally, grouping them within elements designated with `role="radiogroup"` linked to their title, and assigning `role="radio"` and `aria-checked` to individual buttons, ensures screen readers announce selections perfectly.
**Action:** Always group custom interactive segmented/radio button lists under `role="radiogroup"`, map individual items to `role="radio"` with `aria-checked` properties, and utilize standardized themed focus-ring visible styles alongside subtle active tactile scaling transitions.
