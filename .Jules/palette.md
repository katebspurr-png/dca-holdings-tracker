## 2025-03-06 - Enhancing Back Navigation Links
**Learning:** Standard navigation back-buttons or links benefit immensely from explicit icons (like `ArrowLeft`) and tactile scaling transitions (`active:scale-95`). Custom focus ring styles are crucial for dark-themed layouts where standard focus ring colors can fade into the background.
**Action:** Always add explicit left arrow icons, tactile active feedback, and design system focus rings on navigation links.
