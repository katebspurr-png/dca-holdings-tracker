## 2025-06-27 - [Tactile Feedback & A11y in Mobile Navigation]
**Learning:** Adding `active:scale-95` paired with `transition` provides a physical "press" feeling that significantly improves perceived responsiveness on mobile-first interfaces. Combining this with `aria-current="page"` ensures the UI is both delightful and accessible.
**Action:** Always pair interactive state styles (like active scaling) with proper ARIA attributes and focus indicators to maintain a consistent and inclusive experience.
