## 2025-05-15 - Improving Hit Area and Accessibility for Toggle Controls
**Learning:** Using semantic `Label` elements instead of `span` for toggle descriptions significantly improves accessibility by providing a larger, clickable hit area that is naturally associated with the control. However, when a switch has labels on both sides (e.g., Light/Dark), standard `htmlFor` behavior can be counter-intuitive as both labels toggle the state.
**Action:** Use semantic `Label` components with `htmlFor` for accessibility, but combine them with explicit `onClick` handlers and `e.preventDefault()` on the label itself to ensure clicking a specific label sets the intended state rather than just toggling. Adding tactile feedback (`active:scale-95`) to these larger hit areas further enhances the mobile-first UX.
