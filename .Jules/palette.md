## 2025-05-15 - Bottom Tab Bar Accessibility and Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** Adding `aria-current="page"` to active navigation links is a crucial micro-UX and accessibility win that helps screen reader users understand their current location within the app. Pairing this with `active:scale-95` provides immediate tactile feedback for touch users, making the app feel more responsive.
**Action:** Always include ARIA attributes like `aria-current` and micro-interactions like `active:scale-95` when implementing or refining navigation components.
