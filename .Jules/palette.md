## 2025-05-14 - Tactile Feedback Patterns
**Learning:** For mobile-first delight, use `active:scale-95` for small buttons and `active:scale-[0.98]` for larger cards/containers. Always pair with the generic `transition` class to ensure smooth scaling alongside color/opacity changes.
**Action:** Apply these specific scale factors based on element size to maintain a consistent physical feel across the application.
