## 2025-06-27 - Bottom Navigation Accessibility and Tactile Feedback
**Learning:** For mobile-first navigation bars, combining 'aria-current="page"' with 'active:scale-95' tactile feedback and themed 'focus-visible' rings provides a superior, accessible experience.
**Action:** Always apply 'transition active:scale-95' and 'focus-visible:ring-2 focus-visible:ring-stitch-accent/50 focus-visible:ring-offset-2 focus-visible:ring-offset-stitch-bg focus:outline-none' to primary navigation elements.
