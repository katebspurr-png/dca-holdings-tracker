## 2026-03-10 - Enhancing Global Dialog and Sheet Close Buttons for Theme Unity and Touch Target Accessibility
**Learning:** Default Radix/Shadcn close buttons utilize generic rings (ring-ring) and small corner-rounded targets (rounded-sm) which do not align with the custom Google Stitch dashboard styling. They also lack explicit `aria-label` attributes directly on the interactive elements, depending solely on screen-reader helper spans. Standardizing them with fully circular, padded hover targets and custom focus ring properties (`focus-visible:ring-stitch-accent/50`) improves accessibility and provides delightful tactile micro-interactions (`active:scale-95`).
**Action:** Enhance the shared `DialogContent` and `SheetContent` close buttons using fully circular padding, explicit `aria-label`, tactile scale transitions, and stitch-theme offset focus rings.
